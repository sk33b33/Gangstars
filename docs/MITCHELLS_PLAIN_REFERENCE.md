# Mitchells Plain: reference for art and writing

Research notes for making Gangstars look and feel like Mitchells Plain (Cape Town, Cape Flats).
Every fact says where it came from. **Confirmed** = found in a source below. **General knowledge**
= widely known, not confirmed by this round of searching: check it before leaning on it.
The game's crews, people and streets are fictional; this file is about the *place*.

## Place and layout (confirmed)

- About 28 km from central Cape Town, on the Cape Flats between Muizenberg and Khayelitsha.
  Proclaimed for coloured housing in 1971, approved 1974, officially opened April 1976;
  the first residents arrived in **Westridge**. Building reached about 800 houses a month by 1981.
  (Plainsman timeline; Kupi; Wikipedia)
- Planned as a "model suburb" on the **neighbourhood unit concept**: large open spaces, local
  public facilities, **wide arterial roads**. "Neat rows of houses stretch for miles between wide
  thoroughfares." (Wikipedia; Mitchells Plain Alive)
- Western half (Westridge, Rocklands, Portlands) is described as more middle-class; the eastern
  half (Tafelsig, Beacon Valley, Eastridge) as more working-class. (Wikipedia; Mitchells Plain Alive)
- Roads named in sources: **AZ Berman Drive**, Morgenster Road, Vanguard Drive, the M7, the R300.
  A railway line runs north; stations Kapteinsklip, Mitchell's Plain and Lentegeur.
- **Town Centre** is the business district, with a big retail plaza, a shopping centre over and
  around the station, a library and two transport interchanges. The **Promenade** (120+ stores)
  is the biggest mall; Westgate and Watergate are others. Informal traders cluster around the
  interchanges and busy walking routes, selling fruit and vegetables, stationery, spices,
  cosmetics. The taxi rank is a local landmark (30,000+ passenger trips a weekday).
- Nature and open space: **Westridge Gardens** (indigenous fynbos and roses, used for weddings,
  picnics and the Mitchell's Plain Festival, with dunes to look over Westridge from), Wolfgat
  Nature Reserve, **Mnandi Beach** (Blue Flag) to the south on False Bay.

## Houses and streets

Confirmed:
- A mix of **freestanding, semi-detached and duplex** houses. The City Council's planning
  division produced roughly **70 house designs** after surveying buyers' tastes. Most buildings
  are **one- and two-storey brick-and-concrete houses with simple geometric shapes and little
  decoration**. Narrow plots were tried here at scale. Part rented, part sold (sales company
  1978; rental housing east of the railway from 1982). (Kupi; Mitchells Plain Alive; Univ. of Pretoria thesis)
- Residents remember that "all the houses looked the same". (Weekend Argus, 50th anniversary)
- **Backyard dwellings** are common: one-room **corrugated-iron** structures behind small houses,
  with several sharing one yard and often one tap. (Al Jazeera, 2024)
- Informal settlements appeared in the east, notably Eastridge and Tafelsig. Treat with care:
  these are people's homes, not set dressing.

General knowledge (verify before relying on it):
- Over the decades owners personalise the standard house: **burglar bars** and security gates,
  front **boundary walls and palisade fences**, painted exteriors in strong colours, added
  rooms and garages, tiled stoeps, satellite dishes, **Wendy houses** (prefab timber sheds) in
  yards, **pigeon lofts**, washing lines, small gardens with roses, aloes and hedges.
- Face-brick or plastered walls with pitched tile or fibre-cement roofs; flat-roof flats in
  blocks ("courts") are common in the denser parts.

## Landscape and sky

- The Cape Flats were a **sea of moving sand dunes** before roads and planting; the dune
  strandveld and sand fynbos still grow on the edges. Winters are rainy; summers warm and
  **windy** (south-easterly "Cape Doctor" is general knowledge). February is warmest (about
  21 °C average), July coldest (about 12 °C). (Wikipedia; Mitchells Plain Alive)
- **Table Mountain looks different from the Flats: seen "side on", it does not show the famous
  flat-topped postcard profile.** (Calvin University field notes) From Mitchells Plain it is a
  far-off pale-blue ridge to the north-west, on clear days.
- General knowledge: the **Hottentots-Holland / Helderberg mountains** are a jagged blue range
  to the east; **False Bay** and its beaches lie south; the Peninsula mountains
  (Constantiaberg) are a low blue line to the west.

## Culture

Confirmed:
- Many families were moved here from **District Six** under apartheid. (Mitchells Plain Alive; Kaapse Klopse sources)
- The **United Democratic Front was launched at the Rocklands Community Hall (1983)**:
  halls are places of politics and community. Mitchells Plain has a strong activist history.
- **Mosques and churches are central.** Westridge Mosque opened 1982, Rocklands Mosque 1983;
  there is a Town Centre mosque; churches include Apostolic Faith Mission and Lutheran.
- Languages: English and Afrikaans mainly (Cape **Kaaps**), some isiXhosa.
- **Kaapse Klopse** (Cape Minstrels, Tweede Nuwe Jaar, 2 January) and the **Nagtroepe** (Malay
  choirs, New Year's Eve) are Cape Flats traditions; troupes meet in **klopskamers** and
  membership runs in families. The "coon" label is widely seen as a slur and blackface is
  contested: if we show Klopse, use the colourful satin suits, umbrellas and brass bands, not face paint.
- 84 public schools (50 primary, 34 high): school uniform is everyday sight. Local food names
  in sources: chicken tikka, fish dens, Spur, Nando's.
- Sport: football, rugby, darts; youth cups at Lentegeur.

General knowledge (verify): koesisters, gatsbies, bunny chow, curry and roti, braais; pigeon
racing; doeks, curlers and house dresses on aunties; spaza shops, tuck shops and taxi culture;
Cape Malay choir and ghoema drum music.

## Gangs and violence

Sources mention gang violence and that violent gangs are "a way of life" for some. That is
a real hardship for real people, **not** the identity of the place. In the game: crews are
fictional, there is no real gang name, and the world should show the ordinary life (school, shops,
mosque, church, soccer, washing on the line) at least as strongly as the crime.

## What this means for the game

| Finding | Where it shows up |
|---|---|
| Wide arterial roads, open space | Wide streets, sand lots, big sky; keep sightlines long |
| Table Mountain far off, side-on, blue | `Skyline` (north, hazy, no flat postcard top) |
| Dunes, fynbos, wind, False Bay south | Sand drift, dune grass, tufts, sea band in the skyline |
| Simple brick-and-concrete boxes, little decoration | Houses stay plain; variety comes from owners' additions |
| Owners personalise | Hedges, extensions, bars, dishes, garage doors, murals |
| Backyard dwellings | Corrugated back-yard rooms behind some houses |
| Mosques, churches, halls | Mosque in the Town Centre (more to add); church hall; hall bell |
| School uniforms, hijabs, doeks | Character outfits (headscarves, school bags) |
